VPN Master - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how VPN Master ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use the App. The App is published by Albonik. By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not require you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or payment details.

We do not operate the VPN servers listed in the App and we do not record the websites you visit or the content of your traffic.

Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores a few settings only on your device: whether you finished the introduction screens, your light or dark theme choice, and the last VPN server you selected. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall it.

Third-Party Services

VPN servers: the server list is downloaded from VPN Gate (vpngate.net), a public academic project run by the University of Tsukuba, Japan. The servers are run by volunteers around the world. When you connect, your traffic passes through a server operated by one of these volunteers, and that operator or VPN Gate may keep connection logs under its own policies. We do not control those servers and cannot guarantee their logging practices.

Server checks: to hide servers that are offline, the App sends small test connections to the listed VPN servers before you connect. Those servers can see your IP address when this happens.

Network information: the Network Info screen asks ip-api.com for your public IP address, approximate location, and internet provider so it can show them to you. That service receives your IP address as part of the request.

Speed test and ping: the Speed Test screen exchanges test data with third-party speed test servers, and the ping check contacts Google (google.com). These services see your IP address as with any internet request.

Advertising: the App shows ads through Unity LevelPlay and may use Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for consent where required (for example in the EEA and UK). Advertising partners may collect device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID, IP address and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, and to personalize them if you allow it. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Google Play: if you use Share, Rate Us or More Apps, you are taken to Google Play or the share sheet of your device, which have their own privacy policies.

Permissions

Internet and network state: needed to load servers and connect to the VPN.

VPN service: Android asks for your permission before the App creates a VPN connection. The App uses it only to route your traffic through the server you chose.

Notifications: used to show the ongoing VPN connection notification.

Advertising ID: used by ad partners as described above.

Data Security

VPN connections use the OpenVPN protocol to encrypt traffic between your device and the chosen server. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and because the servers are run by third parties, you should avoid sending highly sensitive information over a free public VPN server.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through an ad or third-party service, please contact us so we can help.

Your Choices

You can disconnect the VPN at any time, clear the App's data, uninstall the App, limit ad personalization in your Android settings, and change your consent choice where the consent form is available.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available inside the App and the "Last updated" date below will change.
